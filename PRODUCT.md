# Essence Product Direction

## Brand

**Essence**  
`A lucid space for thought.`

## Positioning

Essence is a calm, writing-first knowledge workspace for people who think deeply and want less software between themselves and their ideas.

Short pitch:

`A quieter alternative to Notion for writers, researchers, and reflective thinkers.`

## Who It's For

- writers
- researchers
- students
- solo founders
- designers collecting references and ideas
- people who find Notion powerful but mentally heavy

## Core Promise

Essence should feel like a thought environment, not a workspace operating system.

That means:

- less setup
- less interface noise
- stronger writing flow
- elegant personal organization
- reliable local-first behavior

For the visual and interaction rules that support this promise, see [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

## Current Product Pillars

- rich block editing for long-form thinking
- deep nested folders for personal organization
- note links and backlinks for connected thinking
- calm editorial reading and writing layout
- fast note browsing through library, collections, tags, and favorites
- local-first persistence with PostgreSQL-backed sync
- quick switching and keyboard-first navigation

## What We Should Win On

- focus
- speed
- clarity
- writing experience
- personal knowledge management

## What We Should Not Chase Yet

- team collaboration
- complex databases
- enterprise workflow tooling
- heavy automation
- feature sprawl that dilutes the writing experience

## Recommended Roadmap

### v1

- polished block editor interactions
- strong search across notes and folder paths
- keyboard shortcuts
- markdown/json export
- reliable autosave and migration safety

### v1.1

- normalized PostgreSQL schema
- note history UI on top of revision storage
- stronger ranking and linked-note search
- recent and pinned note workflows

### v2

- sync across devices
- full version history and restore
- smart collections
- publish/share selected notes
- knowledge graph or linked-note exploration
