# Essence Pitch Deck

## 1. Title

**Essence**  
**A lucid space for thought.**

A minimalist research and note-taking workspace for students, researchers, writers, and deep thinkers.

Essence helps people capture ideas, structure knowledge, read deeply, and use AI without losing clarity.

---

## 2. The Problem

Modern knowledge tools are powerful, but often mentally heavy.

Students, researchers, and writers are surrounded by:

- scattered notes across files, docs, chats, and PDFs
- bloated productivity tools built for teams, not thought
- AI chat outputs that disappear or remain disconnected from actual notes
- weak distinction between writing, reading, studying, and researching
- poor trust layers around generated content, sources, and revisions

The result: people collect more information, but understand less of it.

---

## 3. The Insight

The next great note-taking product is not a bigger workspace.

It is a calmer thinking environment.

People do not only need a place to store notes. They need a place to:

- return to thoughts
- shape raw material into understanding
- review what matters
- connect ideas over time
- use AI as a quiet assistant, not a noisy replacement for thinking

---

## 4. The Product

**Essence is a writing-first knowledge workspace with an AI Composer for serious learning and research.**

Core product:

- rich block editor
- read mode for returning to notes as essays and articles
- nested folders, collections, tags, favorites, and archive
- backlinks and linked notes
- Markdown and JSON import/export
- version history
- PostgreSQL-backed sync
- AI Composer for drafts, note assistance, outlines, and study/review material

Essence is not for managing everything.  
**It is for understanding anything.**

---

## 5. Why Now

AI has changed how people create information, but not how they organize understanding.

Today, a student or researcher can generate an article, summary, or explanation in seconds. But the workflow after generation is broken:

- outputs live in chat threads
- citations are unclear
- notes are disconnected from source material
- revision history is weak
- reading and study workflows are separate from writing tools

Essence brings AI into the note itself, where thinking actually continues.

---

## 6. Target Users

Primary users:

- university students
- graduate researchers
- independent researchers
- writers and essayists
- analysts and knowledge workers
- reflective thinkers who find Notion too heavy

Early adopter wedge:

**People who read, write, and synthesize long-form material every week.**

---

## 7. Market Positioning

Essence sits between minimalist note apps and heavy workspace platforms.

| Product Type | Strength | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Notes / Google Keep | quick capture | weak research structure |
| Notion | flexible workspace | heavy setup and cognitive load |
| Obsidian | powerful linked notes | intimidating for mainstream users |
| AI chat tools | fast generation | disconnected from long-term knowledge |
| Essence | calm structure plus AI-assisted thinking | focused on students, researchers, and writers |

Positioning line:

**A calmer alternative to Notion for people who think, write, and research deeply.**

---

## 8. Product Differentiation

Essence wins through restraint and trust.

Differentiators:

- editorial, reading-first design instead of dashboard-heavy productivity UI
- AI Composer that writes into structured notes, not disposable chat threads
- read mode plus edit mode for both writing and returning to knowledge
- deep folders and tags without database complexity
- versioning for intellectual work over time
- import/export for ownership and portability
- future source-aware research mode for citations, claims, and verification

The product feels less like software administration and more like a quiet room for thought.

---

## 9. AI Composer

Composer is Essence's AI layer.

Current capabilities:

- generate essays, articles, research topics, and quotes from a topic
- continue writing inside an active note
- clarify selected blocks
- create outlines
- generate study questions
- insert AI output as editable Essence blocks

Future capabilities:

- Research Pack: claims, assumptions, open questions, citation gaps
- Study Pack: flashcards, key terms, quiz questions, review summaries
- Source Mode: summarize PDFs, URLs, papers, and lecture transcripts
- Literature Search Prompts: generate precise search queries for further research

Principle:

**AI should support thinking, not replace it.**

---

## 10. Business Model

Essence can monetize through subscription plans tied to sync, AI workflow, research depth, and storage.

### Free

For lightweight personal use.

- local workspace
- basic notes, folders, tags
- Markdown/JSON import-export
- limited Composer usage

### Plus

For serious students, writers, and personal knowledge workers.

- full cloud sync
- higher Composer limits
- version history
- Study Pack
- advanced import/export

### Research

For researchers, analysts, and graduate students.

- source-aware notes
- PDF/article ingestion
- Research Pack
- citation gap detection
- larger context windows
- larger storage

### Team / Lab

For classrooms, labs, and small research groups.

- shared workspaces
- shared source libraries
- admin controls
- team billing
- export and compliance controls

---

## 11. Pricing Direction

Initial pricing hypothesis:

- **Free:** $0
- **Plus:** $8-12/month
- **Research:** $18-25/month
- **Team / Lab:** $12-20/user/month

Revenue thesis:

Students are price-sensitive, but researchers, writers, analysts, and labs will pay for reliable synthesis, source-aware AI, and a calm long-term knowledge base.
